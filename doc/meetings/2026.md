### Associate Member Representative

Naomi Washington informed the Governing Board that a suggestion was made during the All Member Meeting to explore the addition of an Associate Member Representative as a voting Governing Board position. Washington noted this model is used by other foundations/projects and would function similarly to the General Member Representative. For governance, this would require an official charter change and two-thirds approval by the Governing Board, which prompted a discussion on whether the board was interested in pursuing the idea.

A board member supported the proposal, noting that since associate members typically cannot afford a premier-level board seat, this would grant them access to the board, and emphasized the need to consider mechanisms for keeping this group engaged. Another board member added that since the majority of the project's funding used to come from academic sources, this would help that group feel they can support the project in multiple ways, by joining the discussion and through marketing how to support the project.  The board member further noted that academic institutions with grant needs can frame their support as benefiting Jupyter, and they could gain access to Linux Foundation resources for routing their funds into the foundation through charities, further keeping that category of members engaged.
